# Writing style for Meridian content

These rules apply to every reading in `build/data/content/**/*.json` (narrative
paragraphs, tips, fun facts, comprehension checks, SAQ/LEQ/FRQ text, etc.):

- **No em dashes.** Use a period, comma, or colon instead. Rewrite the
  sentence rather than substituting a hyphen or `--`.
- **Keep vocabulary plain.** Don't reach for a complicated or SAT-style word
  when a simpler one says the same thing, unless the word itself is
  required vocabulary for the course's exam (verify against the actual
  College Board CED before assuming something is required).
- Content should read as clear and direct, not padded. These are AP
  students reading on a phone, not an academic paper.
